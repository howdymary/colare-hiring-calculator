How the model works

Stage hours = cohort × participation × minutes × interviewers ÷ 60.

Proposed hours = retained stage hours + review + exceptions + setup + calibration + other added work.

Capacity released = selected stage hours − added work.

Labor-capacity value = selected hours × their rates − added hours × their rates.

Value after costs = labor-capacity value − Colare cost − other incremental cash costs.

Economic ROI = (selected labor value − total incremental investment) ÷ total incremental investment. Investment includes added labor value, Colare cost and other incremental costs. It must be known and greater than zero.

No context-switching multiplier, default salary, measured result, time-to-hire prediction or candidate-quality improvement is assumed. Counts/rates must describe comparable roles, quality requirements and periods.
