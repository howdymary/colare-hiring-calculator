How it’s calculated

Stage hours = cohort × participation × minutes × interviewers ÷ 60.

Proposed hours = retained stage hours + review + exceptions + setup + calibration + other added work.

Time saved = hours replaced − added work.

Value of time saved = selected hours × their rates − added hours × their rates.

Value after costs = value of time saved − Colare fees − other cash costs.

ROI (%) = value after costs ÷ added costs × 100. Added costs include extra staff time, Colare fees and other cash costs. ROI requires known costs greater than zero.
